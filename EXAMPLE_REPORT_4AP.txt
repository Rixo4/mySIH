NOTES FOR CLINICIAN:

1. MECHANISM: 4-AP blocks voltage-gated potassium channels, preventing
   repolarization and extending action potentials. This is the OPPOSITE
   of therapeutic suppression.

2. HYPEREXCITABILITY: Instead of reducing firing, 4-AP INCREASES firing
   dramatically. The network becomes hyperactive and prone to synchronized
   bursting (seizures).

3. CRITICAL FINDING: Unlike Na-blockers which suppress activity, K-blockers
   enhance neuronal excitability. The excitation score of 78% and seizure
   risk of 87% indicate CONTRAINDICATION.

4. THERAPEUTIC WINDOW ABSENT: There is no dose range where 4-AP provides
   therapeutic benefit without triggering seizures. Seizure risk emerges
   at doses as low as 3 µM, well below doses producing appreciable effects.

5. NETWORK INSTABILITY: The network irregularity index (NII) increases
   dramatically, indicating loss of network coherence and emergence of
   chaotic dynamics. This is characteristic of epileptiform activity.

6. SYNCHRONIZATION INCREASE: Instead of reducing network synchronization
   (which would be stabilizing), 4-AP causes massive increase in neuronal
   synchronization. Neurons fire together in abnormal bursting patterns.

7. CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE: Although 4-AP has been investigated for certain
   conditions (e.g., Multiple Sclerosis, spinal cord injury), its use as
   a seizure treatment is absolutely contraindicated. This compound would
   INCREASE rather than DECREASE seizure risk.

8. RESEARCH CONTEXT: 4-AP is valuable as a model for understanding how
   K-channel dysfunction can lead to epilepsy. Many inherited epilepsy
   syndromes involve gain-of-function or loss-of-function mutations in
   K-channel genes.

9. ENGINE PERFORMANCE NOTE: This example demonstrates the critical
   importance of the K-channel hyperexcitability detection rule. Without
   this rule, 4-AP might be misclassified as a suppressant (suppression=0%)
   rather than a hyperexcitant. The engine correctly identifies 4-AP as
   dangerous based on: firing rate INCREASE + K-channel blockade = HIGH RISK.
