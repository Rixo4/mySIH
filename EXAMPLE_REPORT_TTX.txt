NOTES FOR CLINICIAN:

1. EFFICACY: TTX is 100% effective at blocking sodium channels, but this
   effectiveness comes at the cost of complete loss of function at all
   practical doses.

2. LACK OF THERAPEUTIC WINDOW: There is NO separation between therapeutic
   effect and toxicity. The smallest detectable dose causes complete
   neural silencing.

3. MECHANISM: TTX's complete and irreversible sodium channel blockade
   provides no selectivity for diseased vs. healthy tissue.

4. CLINICAL IMPLICATIONS: TTX is unsuitable as a therapeutic drug due to:
   - Extreme potency (effective at nanomolar concentrations)
   - Complete loss of function at all doses
   - No therapeutic window for safe dosing
   - All neural activity is eliminated

5. RESEARCH USE ONLY: TTX is valuable as a research tool to study sodium
   channel dependence, but has no therapeutic application.

6. HISTORICAL NOTE: TTX has been explored as an analgesic, but the complete
   functional blockade makes it unsuitable for this purpose.
